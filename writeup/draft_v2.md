# Parameter Golf: Six Weeks to Build the Best LLM

---

In March 2026, OpenAI released a public competition with a deceptively simple premise: train the best language model you can, but it has to fit in 16,000,000 bytes, and you only get 10 minutes of training time on 8 H100s. Call it parameter golf — every byte counts, every second counts.

What followed over the next six weeks looked, from the outside, like just another competitive coding challenge. It turned out to be something more: techniques stacking on each other in ways nobody planned, innovations that shouldn't have worked but did, controversial submissions that looked like miracles, mayhem on the last day, and a picture-perfect finish. This post goes through some of the highlights — both the technical and the dramatic.
