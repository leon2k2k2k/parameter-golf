# Parameter Golf: Six Weeks to Build the Best LLM

---

In March 2026, OpenAI released a public competition with a deceptively simple premise: train the best language model you can, but it has to fit in 16,000,000 bytes, and you only get 10 minutes of training time on 8 H100s. Call it parameter golf — every byte counts, every second counts.

What followed over the next six weeks was not what anyone expected. The leaderboard did not converge smoothly. It lurched. Submissions appeared that seemed to shatter the field entirely, scoring far beyond what careful engineering could explain. Investigations followed. Disqualifications came. And on the final day, a quiet reshuffling left the door wide open for one of the cleanest finishes the competition could have asked for.

This post covers what happened: the engineering, the controversy, and the lesson hiding underneath all of it.
