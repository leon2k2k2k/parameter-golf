## Hypothesis

Per-pass (γ, β) conditioning on the attn and MLP residual branches, tested in isolation against the 050 base (no recip-init, no iter-embeds). Each loop pass learns a distinct scale/shift on the residual contribution, giving passes differentiated roles without changing weight sharing. Plain elementwise ops — natively traceable by dynamo, no `@allow_in_graph` hack needed (unlike the failed 047D approach). Init: γ=1, β=0 (transparent start).

Spec 050 (`f5b8af8`) pre-quant EMA val_bpb ~1.067. Isolated test: only LOOP_ADABN=1 vs 050 base.
