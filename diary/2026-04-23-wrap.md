## TTT carry adaptation

### 033

Low-LR TTT adaptation of frozen alpha/beta was effectively neutral:

- tiny alpha movement
- beta unchanged
- result difference was negligible

### 033b

High-LR TTT adaptation of frozen alpha/beta answered the real question:

- alpha moved a lot
- beta also moved materially
- final result got worse

So the conclusion is now fairly clean:

- alpha/beta are movable under TTT
- but strong movement hurts
- this line is low priority unless the parameterization changes

## 034 / 034b status

### 034

`034` is the frozen `031A` line.

Important correction made today:

- the spec originally omitted explicit 3-phase TTT settings
- that would have defaulted to `PHASED_TTT_NUM_PHASES=1`
- the frozen branch now explicitly pins:
  - `TTT_ENABLED=1`
  - `PHASED_TTT_PREFIX_DOCS=2000`
  - `PHASED_TTT_NUM_PHASES=3`

So if an older `034` checkpoint already exists, the correct recovery path is:

- do not retrain just to fix TTT
- hotstart from the saved checkpoint and rerun the post-training TTT path

### 034b

- `034bB` = same hotstart path, but direct-carry becomes learnable during TTT

The learnable probe is slightly stronger now:
